# Product

## Platform

Web, including mobile browsers. Next.js App Router renders the frontend and Django serves the catalogue, learner records and assessments.

## Users and purpose

Start with SSC CGL Tier I aspirants who already practise previous-year questions. Help them understand where they lose marks, decide what to practise next, and check the same skill on fresh questions. New visitors should be able to try a question before creating an account.

## Positioning

SSC Pitara connects the paper archive, short skill lessons, topic practice, mock reports and study recommendations. The archive also serves other SSC exams. Use published inventory, approved materials and demonstrated feature behaviour as proof.

## Operating context and constraints

Keep entry points usable on small screens and with a keyboard. Public papers and lessons give guests a way to explore. Accounts save activity and unlock personalised study recommendations. Use the current release and access rules when describing individual features. The beginner foundation curriculum is in development.
